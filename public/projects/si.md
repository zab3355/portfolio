## What's Supplemental Instruction?

Supplemental Instruction at RIT is facilitated by peer leaders who have previously completed the course. Through structured, one-hour sessions that encourage collaboration, students identify what to learn and how to learn it. International data suggests that students who regularly attend SI sessions are more likely to earn a higher grade in the course.

---

## Collaboration and Learning

Throughout my time as a Supplemental Instructor, I learned the importance of collaboration when it comes to how certain people may learn something better and how to create a variety of activities. Other Supplemental Instructors shared games and activities to engage students — matching games, programming challenges, and more! Even instructors from adjacent courses shared concepts that helped students in sessions.

---

## Communication

Explaining code or complicated processes to peers can be difficult, especially when someone is unfamiliar with a specific topic. As a Supplemental Instructor, we're faced with this challenge every day. This experience has and will continue to help throughout my career — I've had to explain complex work on multiple occasions. It's important to keep concepts simplistic and thoughts organized when crafting explanations.

Being a role model for students was also key to recruiting multiple new Supplemental Instructors to continue helping the student body.
