## Angular Leaderboard

During the first week at Sandbox Union, tasked with creating a leaderboard for a gaming tournament at the gaming bar PlayerzZone for their Madden tournament. Decided to use Google Firebase as the database and have an AngularJS frontend. The scoreboard also had an admin section, where a user could adjust scores, add new teams, and edit their teams.

**GitHub:** [angularleaderboard](https://github.com/zab3355/angularleaderboard)
