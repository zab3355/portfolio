## About Weather Watcher

Created a Node.JS application called Weather Watcher that figures out what a user should wear depending on precipitation and temperature. Using the Google Maps API and OpenWeatherMaps API, it provides a list of clothing recommendations based on specific weather conditions — below 40°F: long sleeve; above 40°F: short sleeve; heavy jacket for snow/ice storms. FontAwesome icons are included to show weather changes.

---

## How It Works

This application tracks what you should wear based on your location. You can either search by city or use your current location. Due to being hosted on Heroku, it might take around 10–15 seconds initially to receive results. Screenshots are available in the project.
